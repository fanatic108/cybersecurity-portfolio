Galaxy Motors Re-Identification Investigation

Scenario

This investigation focused on re-identifying individuals by correlating information gathered from multiple data sources, including website content, forum posts, email evidence, and publicly available information.

Objective

The goal was to determine the identities of individuals through evidence correlation while documenting the methodology used to reach each conclusion.

Investigation Methodology

  • Analyzed Galaxy Motors website content.
  • Reviewed forum discussions and user posts.
  • Examined email server evidence.
  • Correlated hobbies, professions, locations, and personal details.
  • Validated findings against available records.

Lessons Learned

This project demonstrated how seemingly harmless pieces of information can be combined to reveal identities. It reinforced the importance of privacy awareness, OSINT techniques, evidence correlation, and analytical thinking during investigations.
